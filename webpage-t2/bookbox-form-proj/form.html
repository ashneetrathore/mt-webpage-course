Welcome to the Rapunzel Reads Book Box! These boxes specially include items based on your favorite genre in books. The box includes one book and many other trinkets that all fit the theme of the genres you choose every month. This box was created to help you explore a genre that you like to read or find a genre that you will love to read. We specialize in hand picking young adult top sellers, however we can occasionally choose an adult or even a middle grade novel. Fill out this form, including your name, address, genre of book, and goodie list, to order your box! Shipping is free for first time customers, but otherwise it is $8.00. Then, click "Order Now!" and your order is placed.
