Minimalism, at its core, is about living intentionally with only the materialistic possessions that fulfill our needs or ones that we value the most. Minimalism is not about living with less, but living with less can be a result of minimalism. Minimalists typically lead uncluttered lives, surrounding themselves with simple environments in order to live a more happier, orderly life. Minimalist wardrobes are referred to as capsule wardrobes, which are a collection of a few essential items of clothing that are not incorporated into the fast-fashion trend, meaning they can be augmented with any seasonal pieces without going out of fashion. It's hard to go through stores and scavenge through all the clothes to find the essentials you want. Our company offers such pieces to slowly build your capsule wardrobe and start your minimalist journey, without going through the mess of shopping at companies that target your shopping weaknesses.
