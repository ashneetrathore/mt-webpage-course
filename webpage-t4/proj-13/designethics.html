The fonts I used in the websites are cursive Dokdo, cursive Pacifico, monospace Roboto, Amatic SC, Inconsolata, and a sans-serif Source Sans Pro. I obtained these fonts from the Google Fonts online website. I used these specific fonts because I thought they all complimented each other in an aesthetically pleasing way, and also because I like Google Fonts better than saving an image that has a specific font or word art from the CoolText website. I do have rights to use these fonts because they are published with already existing licenses that allow users to use them on any website.

I used a wallpaper image that had a pastel book design as a background for all my web pages on my website. For my bookmark products, I used all jpg images and one jxr image. I got these images from Google Images. I needed these images because my customers should have a visual piece to look at to decide if the bookmark is what they want, and to make the website more aesthetically pleasing with a pastel background. The panda, Harry Potter, European attraction, and food bookmarks are products from independently owned Etsy businesses and the cat and watercolor flower bookmarks are from the Pinterest page of artists, which means they aren't royalty free. The watercolor/ succulent watercolor bookmarks are from a blog called Sarica Studio, the succulent bookmarks are from Fox+Hazel business, and the bunny bookmark is from It's Always Autumn blog. These artists design their own bookmarks and give ideas to their audience on bookmark designs. I cannot use this image without permission because it is the artists' design and it had a watermark before I edited it. The space, European vintage, and Albus Dumbledore bookmarks are from websites that feature the work from other businesses, but those websites have permission to use those pictures. I would have to pay money for all these images to use these on my website. If I were to actually use these images ethically and fairly if I was hired to create this website, I would have to contact these independent businesses and request permission to use them. The background of the website is royalty free because it's free wallpaper without a copyright or watermark.
